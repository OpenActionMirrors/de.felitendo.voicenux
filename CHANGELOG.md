# Changelog

What each release brings, newest first.

## v0.4.0

_30 October 2026_

Welcome to Voicenux for OpenDeck `v0.4.0`! Find sounds and voices by saying their name. The new keys need Voicenux 0.4.0.

### Highlights

- **Say a Sound**: hold the key and say a sound, like "vine boom". It finds the sound most people liked on MyInstants and puts it on the key
- Not the right sound? Press the key twice quickly for the one with the next most likes
- **Say a Voice**: hold the key and say one of your voices, like "Darth Vader", to use it
- A light runs round the key while it looks, and the settings of the key show how it works
- **Sound (Dial)**: turn to pick a sound, press to play it. Only favorites if you like

## v0.3.0

_27 October 2026_

Welcome to Voicenux for OpenDeck `v0.3.0`! The plugin is ready for the next Voicenux, which asks before an app may control it.

### Highlights

- While Voicenux asks, the keys show **Allow?** and wait for your answer
- If you said no, the keys show **Not allowed**, and the settings of a key show how to let the plugin in

## v0.2.1

_19 October 2026_

A cleanup inside the plugin. Your keys and settings work just like before.

## v0.2.0

_19 October 2026_

Welcome to Voicenux for OpenDeck `v0.2.0`! The plugin no longer needs Node.js.

### Highlights

- Install it and it runs, without Node.js
- Runs on ARM64 computers too
- Works on older Linux distributions
- Your keys and settings stay as they are
- Playing a sound on top works with Voicenux 0.2.0

## v0.1.0

_19 October 2026_

Welcome to Voicenux for OpenDeck `v0.1.0`! Control the Voicenux voice changer from your Stream Deck on Linux.

### Highlights

- Voice keys with the picture of their voice and a frame while it is in use
- Sound keys that play from the start, on top, or play and stop, with a frame while they play
- Voice changer, mute, hear myself, background and hear sounds
- Push to talk, push to change your voice, or a voice only while you hold its key
- Random, next and previous voice, out of your favorites if you have some
- A random sound, with a dice or the sound that comes next on the key
- Soundboard volume on a key or a dial, and a key to stop all sounds
- A dial that turns through your voices, with their pictures
- Most keys do one thing on press, another on hold and another on double press
- Keys and settings tell you when Voicenux is off or does not let apps in
- In English and German, following the language of OpenDeck
